Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Wiz Mileage ("the app") is an offline fuel and mileage log for Android, made by Wiz Labs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information you enter

You can enter vehicle details (name, type, fuel, registration number, tank capacity) and fill-up records (date, odometer reading, amount paid, RSP, full-tank flag, fuel brand, note). This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is used only to show your history and insights.

Backups and exports

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's log file in your device backup, under your Google account and Google's terms. The app itself does not upload anything.

When you choose Export to CSV, the app writes a file to the location you pick. When you choose Import CSV, it reads only the file you pick. What happens to an exported file after that is up to you.

Sharing logs with another phone

You can choose to share a vehicle's log as a QR code or a share code. The code contains a copy of the fill-ups you choose to share (dates, odometer readings, amounts, RSP, brand, notes and vehicle details). It is created on your phone and passed directly to the other person: shown on your screen or sent through an app you pick, such as WhatsApp. Nothing is uploaded to Wiz Labs or anyone else. Anyone who receives the code can read the log in it, so share it only with people you trust.

Permissions

Camera (optional): requested only when you choose to scan a share QR code. Camera frames are analysed on the device to read the QR code and are never saved or sent. You can deny it and paste a code instead.

The app has no internet permission and cannot access your location, contacts or other apps' data.

Deleting your data

Delete individual fill-ups or vehicles inside the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything from the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Fuel brand names

IndianOil, Bharat Petroleum, Hindustan Petroleum, Nayara Energy, Jio-bp and Shell are trademarks of their owners. They are used only so you can label where you filled up. Wiz Labs is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Changes

Contact
